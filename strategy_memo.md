1. Focus on TV Shows & 1–2 Season Formats: Movies make up most of the catalog, but  TV shows have grown strongly in recent years and 1-season shows help viewers start easily. We should test new shows as 1–2 season runs and renew the ones that perform well.

2. Prioritize Core Ratings (TV-MA, TV-14) & Top Genres: TV-MA and TV-14 lead the catalog, while International Movies, Dramas, and Comedies are the top genres. Investing more in strong dramas, comedies, and international titles can boost viewer engagement.

3. Keep Movie Runtime Around ~90–100 Minutes: Movie lengths have a median of about 98 minutes. Aiming for roughly 90–105 minutes for movies and originals fits the sweet spot for smooth completion.
